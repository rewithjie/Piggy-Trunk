Engineered for Agricultural Precision

A comprehensive suite of tools eliminating manual paperwork, double allocations, and blind investments.

📈

Dynamic Capital & Feed Expense Tracking

Approved feed requisitions are automatically valued against current inventory prices and deducted from the batch's initial capital in real time.

🐷

Strict 1:1 Batch Assignment

Enforces zero double-allocation business logic. A hog raiser can only manage one active batch at a time to ensure maximum livestock care and accountability.

🛡️

Immutable Audit Trail

Every product restock, inventory price change, approval, and user suspension is permanently logged with the authenticated user's email and timestamp.

🔔

Push Notifications & Requisitions

Hog raisers submit stock requests from mobile. Administrators approve requests from the web dashboard with auto-deduction and instant push updates.

📊

Demand Forecasting & POS

Built-in Point of Sale for retail pork cuts with moving-average demand forecasting to predict stock replenishment needs before shortages occur.

💼

Transparent Investor Portfolios

External partners can track their capital allocations across hog batches, view growth metrics, and audit livestock health without visiting the farm.

End-to-End Swine Lifecycle Workflow

From batch creation to harvest and retail sales in 4 seamless stages.

STAGE 01

Batch Setup & Allocation

Admin configures a new batch (Fattening or Sow), assigns an approved Hog Raiser, and registers initial capital investment.

STAGE 02

Feed Requisition & Growth

Raiser requests feeds via mobile app. Admin approves with 1 click; warehouse inventory deducts, and batch expense tracks automatically.

STAGE 03

Harvest & Retail POS

Mature hogs are harvested and transferred to retail inventory. Cashiers sell meat cuts through the mobile POS system.
